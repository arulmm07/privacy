Delete Account

If you would like to delete your account and associated data from the Opthal application, you can request account deletion using the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Opthal application.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open your Profile or Account section.
  4. Select the account deletion option.
  5. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted?

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Your account and the personal information associated with it will be deleted from our systems, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.

Need Help?

If you are unable to delete your account through the application, please contact the application support team using the support contact provided in the Google Play Store listing.
