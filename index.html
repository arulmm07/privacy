Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Welcome to Opthal. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Opthal application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the application may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Account and authentication information
  • Quiz and viva practice activity
  • Application preferences and settings

We only collect information that is necessary to provide and improve the application's functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate your login
  • Provide quiz and viva practice functionality
  • Maintain your application preferences
  • Improve application functionality and reliability
  • Provide support when required

3. Authentication and Account Security

The application uses authentication mechanisms to protect user accounts. Passwords are not displayed to administrators through normal application interfaces.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential.

4. Microphone and Voice Features

If you use voice-based viva functionality, the application may request microphone permission so that you can provide spoken responses.

Microphone access is used only when the relevant voice functionality is being used. You can manage microphone permissions through your device settings.

5. Data Storage

Account and application-related information may be stored on servers used to provide the application's services.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or improve the application.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for functions such as authentication, hosting, analytics, or other technical services. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention

Account information is retained while your account is active or for as long as necessary to provide the application's services.

When an account deletion request is submitted, associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized where applicable, subject to legitimate legal or operational requirements.

9. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the account deletion process provided by the application.

You can find the account deletion instructions on our Account Deletion page.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for educational use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
